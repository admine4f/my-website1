1. Who we are

E4F Web3 Exchange ("E4F", "we", "us") operates the Telegram Mini App available through the Telegram bot @E4F_Web3_mining_bot and the web address https://my-website-rouge-omega.vercel.app (the "Service"). This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used and the choices you have. By using the Service you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

a) From Telegram

When you open the Mini App, Telegram provides us with your Telegram user ID, username, first and last name, profile photo link and language code. We verify this data on our server so that nobody can act on your behalf.

b) Activity in the app

  • Farming/mining sessions, daily check-ins, spins, gift boxes and completed tasks;
  • Referral information (your referral code and who invited you);
  • In-app balances and reward history;
  • Messages you send to our support.

c) Advertising records

When you watch a rewarded ad we record that an ad session started and finished (ad network used, session ID, time) so that the reward can be credited correctly and abuse can be prevented. We do not receive the personal data of advertisers' customers.

d) Technical and security data

IP address, device and browser information, and a hashed device identifier that helps us detect duplicate or fake accounts, bots and other abuse. Our servers also keep normal security and error logs.

e) Wallet details (only in the future)

If payment features are enabled later and you choose to use them, we may need a wallet address and transaction details. We do not collect these today for payments (see section 6).

We do not ask for your ID card, passport, bank or card details, private messages, contacts, phone number, precise location or access to your device storage. Please do not send such information to our support team.

3. How we use information

  • to run the Service, create your account and credit your rewards;
  • to operate referrals, daily limits and daily resets (00:00 UTC) and to calculate your Trusted Miner streak;
  • to show rewarded ads and confirm that an ad was viewed;
  • to protect the Service and other users from fraud, multiple accounts, bots and abuse;
  • to answer your support requests;
  • to maintain, secure and improve the Service and to meet legal obligations.

4. Advertising and third-party ad networks

The Service shows optional rewarded ads supplied by third-party ad networks. These currently include RichAds, Monetag and OnClickA, and may include Adsgram once our application is approved. Their scripts (SDKs) run inside the app page and may collect technical data such as IP address, device and browser information, advertising identifiers or cookies, and ad interactions, as described in each network's own privacy policy.

  • Watching ads is optional; you decide whether to watch an ad to receive a reward.
  • We do not intentionally share your name, username or balances with ad networks.
  • We configure ads to be shown inside the app. We do not control the content of third-party ads.
  • We do not sell your personal data to ad networks or to anyone else.

5. Who we share information with

  • Service providers that host and operate the Service, such as database and hosting providers (for example Supabase and Vercel), and Telegram itself;
  • Advertising partners, only to the extent described in section 4;
  • Authorities or other parties when required by law or to protect our rights, users and the Service;
  • a successor, if the Service is transferred as part of a merger or sale of the business.

6. Virtual balances and payments

  • At this time we have not enabled payments. The Service does not accept real-money deposits or purchases and does not pay out real money.

  • Balances displayed in the app (including E4F and USDT-denominated credits) are virtual in-app credits. They are not bank money, they have no guaranteed monetary value, and they cannot currently be withdrawn.
  • We plan to consider enabling payment features after the planned listing of the E4F coin. This is only a plan, not a promise, and no date or value is guaranteed.
  • Before any payment feature is switched on we will update this policy and inform users in the app or through the bot.

Nothing in the Service is financial, investment or legal advice.

7. Account verification (Trusted Miner)

  • Zero ID Card / No KYC Required. We do not ask for identity documents and we do not take deposits to verify an account.
  • Mine at least once every day (UTC time) for 120 days in a row. On the 120th day your profile automatically shows ✅ Trusted Miner, which means you are a verified user and not a robot.
  • To calculate your streak we use the dates of your mining sessions. If you miss a day, the count starts again and the badge is not given.
  • If you cannot complete the 120 days you may ask our Support team for a manual review. A manual review fee of 120 E4F (virtual in-app points) applies.

8. Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active and as long as needed to provide the Service, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. When you ask us to delete your data we delete or anonymise it, except for records we must keep for security, fraud prevention or legal reasons. Technical logs are kept for a limited time.
